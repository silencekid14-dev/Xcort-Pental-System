Physics rules simulated:
• Data packets travel at finite speed derived from claimed latency
• ALUs starve if data has not arrived (bandwidth × latency product)
• PRAM 3.07 TB/s is a hard ceiling — excess demand → queue / idle
• PGPU STET under divergent load forces serialisation → utilisation drops
• DTET keeps independent layers → utilisation stays high
• Throlter “off” causes thermal derate of clock
